Physics • Motion

Why does an object keep moving after a force is removed?

Asked by Rahul

Answer: According to Newton's First Law of Motion, an object continues in its state of motion unless an external unbalanced force acts on it.
Biology • Cell

Why is the cell called the basic unit of life?

Asked by Ananya

Answer: All living organisms are made up of cells, and the basic functions of life are carried out by cells.
